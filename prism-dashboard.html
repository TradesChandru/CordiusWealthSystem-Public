HOW TO USE PRISM

PRISM watches gold (XAUUSD) 24 hours a day, analysing 14 key price levels and 5 technical indicators across 5 timeframes. When conditions align, it tells you exactly what to do.

The three things PRISM always tells you:

1. Buy, Sell, or Wait  ·  2. Where to take profit  ·  3. Where to cut the loss

Your job: follow the signal, respect the stop, exit at the target. PRISM does the analysis. You execute.

SIGNAL TYPES

⚡⚡ STRONG — Compound Rejection (73%)
Three things at once: a bounce off a key level, hidden momentum strength, and a pullback pattern. 73 of 100 reach the 5pt target within 1H. Enter immediately, exit in 1H.

⚡ SIGNAL — Level Rejection (72%)
Gold touched an important level and bounced. 72 of 100 reached target within the first hour. Enter in direction of bounce, exit in 1H.

ℹ BREAK — Informational (37%)
Gold broke through a level. Often reverses quickly — context, not a trade. Do not hold > 1H.

⏳ WAIT — No Setup
Trend mixed or no level interaction. PRISM updates every 10s. Wait — a signal will come.

WHEN TO TRADE — SESSIONS

NY (13:00–22:00 UTC): BEST
Highest accuracy for rejections. Institutional flow confirms level tests.

ASIAN (22:00–06:00 UTC): GOOD
More breakouts, smaller moves. Good for momentum breaks.

LONDON (06:00–13:00 UTC): GOOD
Most rejections overall. Good for level tests at open.

Exit rule: Compound & Level signals exit within 1H. Rare swing signals up to 24H. Never hold intraday through a session change.

THE FINDINGS — VALIDATED STATS

15 months of gold data (Apr 2025 – Jul 2026), 7,177 signal readings, 13,789 level interactions.

Rejection accuracy (5pts / 1H):
Weekly ST 88.9% · Daily ST 75.0% · Round 100 74.4%
Prior day low 71.6% · Prior wk close 70.1% · VWAP-D 66.2% · 1H ST 66.7%

Hidden divergence adds edge: pullback + divergence 72.3% vs no divergence 62.4% (+9.9pp).

Volume: high-vol 79.4% · normal 70.2% · low 40.5% (avoid). Session: NY 70.0% · ASIAN 68.5% · LONDON 67.2%.

WHAT PRISM DOESN'T DO

PRISM is not a crystal ball — it gives probability edges, not certainties.

• 73% accuracy means 27 of 100 signals lose. Always use the stop.
• Built for 1H decisions — not scalping, not multi-day holds.
• XAUUSD only. Don't apply signals to other instruments.
• 15-min delay on some level events may miss news spikes — stand aside during high-impact news.
• Past accuracy ≠ future results. Re-validation at 6 months.

METHODOLOGY (PO REVIEW)

Supertrend(10,3) × 5 TF · RSI(14) + hidden divergence × 4 TF · VWAP (D/W) · 14 price levels · level interaction scoring (break/reject × volume × session).

Scoring: signal_score (−10..+10) + level_interaction_score (−10..+10) = combined_conviction (−20..+20). Outcome: MFE/1H, 5pts = win.
